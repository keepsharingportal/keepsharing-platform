### 1.3 The "First 30 Days in the River Region" Checklist — P0

A practical, scannable, tactile checklist for newcomer families. Lives in the print issue and on the web.

**Suggested checklist items (refine with DeAnne):**

Within Week 1
- Register your child with the school district (public) or contact admissions (private)
- Establish a pediatrician — many practices have wait times for new patients
- Update your driver's license (Alabama 30-day rule)
- Get utilities transferred
- Find your nearest grocery, drugstore, and urgent care

Within Month 1
- Visit local libraries (huge family resource often missed)
- Attend at least one community event — farmer's market, story time, park day
- Identify a faith community if relevant
- Sign kids up for any active sports league or activity (timing matters by season)
- Find a local coffee shop or restaurant where you'd take grandparents

Within Month 2-3
- Connect with a parent group, mom's group, or church small group
- Try at least 3 family activities you've never done
- Establish a dentist (kid + adult)
- Visit at least 2 neighborhoods you didn't move into so you know the area
- Subscribe to local newsletters (River Region Parents, neighborhood association, school)

**Format:** Printed as a 2-page tear-out spread or single full page. On web, becomes a downloadable PDF + interactive checkable list (Phase 2 web feature, P1 enhancement).

**Production:** I draft, DeAnne edits, you approve.

## Section 4 — Featured Listing Design Standards

### 4.1 Free Listing format
- Business name, address, phone, website
- 1-line description (15 words max)
- No logo, no photo

### 4.2 Featured Listing format (Print)
- Business name in bold larger type
- Logo (small, 1-color)
- Address, phone, website, hours
- 1-2 sentence editorial blurb (NOT marketing copy)
- "Why we love them" or "What families say" line
- Categories/specialties
- Print ad cross-reference if applicable ("See our full ad on page X")

### 4.3 Featured Listing format (Web)
- Logo + cover photo
- Address, phone, website, social links
- Hours
- 1-2 paragraph editorial blurb
- Photo gallery (3-5 images)
- "Currently accepting new patients/clients" badge if relevant
- Google Maps embed
- Click-to-call, click-to-website
- Quote pulled from advertiser interview (Section 6)
- Categories/specialties

### 4.4 Section Sponsor format (Print) — Schools and Pediatric Care for 2026
- Top of section, full-width
- "Schools section sponsored by [Sponsor name]"
- Sponsor's logo
- Editorial intro paragraph (200-300 words) that ties sponsor's expertise to the section topic
- Full-page ad on facing page or within the same spread
- Featured Listing within the section directory below editorial

### 4.5 Section Sponsor format (Web)
- Top of section, full-width banner
- Sponsor's logo and tagline
- Editorial intro paragraph
- "Sponsored by" attribution
- Sponsor's profile linked from the banner

## Section 6 — Advertiser Quote Collection

This is the editorial engine. Every category-relevant advertiser is invited to answer 3-5 short questions. Their answers become quotes inside articles, sidebars, and Featured Listings. They're elevated from advertiser to expert.

Three category-tailored questionnaires. Each takes the recipient ~10 minutes to fill out.

### 6.1 Pediatric / Healthcare advertisers — Question prompts

1. What's the single most important thing newcomer families to the River Region should know about establishing pediatric care?
2. What's a question parents always ask you that you wish they'd ask sooner?
3. What's something about [your specialty / practice approach] that you wish more local families knew?
4. What do you love most about practicing medicine for River Region families specifically?
5. (Optional) Is your practice currently accepting new patients?

### 6.2 School advertisers — Question prompts

1. What does a newcomer family typically wish they'd known when starting the school search?
2. What makes [Your School / your district] distinctive for relocating families?
3. What's the timeline a family should expect from inquiry to enrollment?
4. What's a moment from the past school year that made you proud of [Your School / your district]?
5. (Optional) What's one piece of practical advice you'd give a parent visiting schools for the first time?

### 6.3 Service businesses (Real Estate, Photographers, Home Services, etc.) — Question prompts

1. What's something a newcomer family to the River Region should know that nobody tells them about [your industry]?
2. What's a common mistake you see relocating families make that you wish you could help them avoid?
3. What's your favorite thing about working with River Region families?
4. What's a small thing you do for clients that makes a big difference?
5. (Optional) What's your favorite spot in the River Region to recommend to newcomers?
