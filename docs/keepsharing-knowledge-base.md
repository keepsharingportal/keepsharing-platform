# SECTION 1: THE GUIDE SYSTEM

## 1.1 Philosophy — Digital First, Print Second

**The old way (backwards):**
Collect info manually → spreadsheet → some in magazine → VA uploads to WordPress → sits outdated until next year

**The right way:**
Business submits their own listing → Claude API formats it → live on website immediately → best listings selected for print → print is a curated highlight of the full online guide → QR in magazine drives traffic back to full searchable guide

**The core principle:**
Free listings make the guide worth visiting. Paid listings make the guide worth advertising in. You need both.

---

## 1.2 The Three-Tier Listing System

### Tier 0 — Community Listing (FREE)
**Purpose:** Makes the guide comprehensive and trustworthy. Moms trust a guide that includes everything, not just paid advertisers.

**What businesses get:**
- Name, category, city, phone number
- One sentence description
- Ages served
- Website link
- "Call for dates and pricing" default text
- Listed alphabetically within category
- No photo
- No featured placement
- No registration status badge

**What it looks like:** A clean directory entry. Useful. Honest. Not exciting.

**Why it matters strategically:**
- Every free listing is a warm business relationship
- Businesses in the free listing receive the annual renewal sequence in GHL
- When a publisher calls about print advertising, they already have context
- Free listings build the business email list
- Creates competitive tension — free listings look clearly inferior to paid

---

### Tier 1 — Enhanced Listing ($175/season)
**Purpose:** Businesses that want real visibility without a full print commitment.

**What businesses get:**
- Full description up to 300 words
- Up to 3 photos
- Specific dates and pricing displayed
- Registration status badge: Open / Waitlist / Full / Opening Soon
- Financial aid available badge
- Age filter match (appears when parents filter by age range)
- Neighborhood tag (appears in local neighborhood filtering)
- Social share optimized (good Open Graph image when moms share)
- "Enhanced" badge within category
- Sorts ABOVE Community listings within category

**Price justification:** $175 one time per season. One registration from a camp charging $200/week pays for it. Easy yes.

**Self-serve purchase:** /summer-fun-guide/upgrade — business finds listing, clicks "Upgrade," pays $175 via Stripe, receives email with link to complete enhanced profile. VA notified in Today screen.

---

### Tier 2 — Advertiser Listing (INCLUDED with print ad)
**Purpose:** Makes print advertising more valuable. This IS the "matching web ad" from the rate card — now given real meaning.

**What businesses get (everything in Enhanced PLUS):**
- Gold border on listing card
- "River Region Parents Advertiser" trust badge
- Priority placement — sorts BEFORE Enhanced listings within category
- Featured on homepage guide section during guide season
- QR code tracking — how many scans their listing receives
- Included in monthly performance report
- Newsletter feature during guide season
- Social spotlight post during guide season

**Cost:** Included with their print ad. No separate charge.

**Pitch to existing advertisers:**
"Your print ad now includes a full enhanced guide listing with photos, dates, and priority placement. Every mom who searches for a camp in your category sees you first. We track every click and include it in your monthly report."

## 1.4 The Self-Update System

**The problem it solves:** You were doing all data entry manually. This puts that work on the businesses.

**How it works:**

Every January — GHL sends to every business in last year's guide:

*"It's time to update your River Region Parents [Guide Name] listing for 2026. Your listing from last year is shown below. Click here to review it, update your dates and prices, and add new photos. Takes less than 5 minutes. Deadline: March 15."*

Link goes to pre-filled form with their existing data. They update what changed. Submit. Goes to VA approval queue. Approved — live on site.

**GHL Follow-Up Sequence:**
- Day 1: Initial update request email sent
- Day 7: If no submission — "Don't miss the deadline" follow-up
- Day 14: If no submission — "Last chance — [date] deadline"
- Day 15: Deadline passes
- Day 16: VA reviews non-submitters
  - Former advertisers → personal call from publisher
  - Community listings → carry over from last year with "Unverified" flag
  - New businesses → remove from guide

**The "Unverified" badge:** Shows on listing: "This listing was not verified for 2026. Call before registering." Honest for readers. Creates urgency for businesses to update next year.

**The Listing Claim System:**
Every listing has "Is this your business?" link. Clicking shows:
- Update your free listing form (pre-filled)
- Upgrade to Enhanced Listing option ($175 via Stripe)
- No human needed for either action

# SECTION 3: CONTENT STRATEGY

## 3.1 The Editorial Philosophy

**One test for every piece of content:**
Can a reader say "I know them" or "that's my community"?

If yes — publish it.
If no — localize it or replace it.

**What we never do:**
Publish generic parenting content that could come from any national magazine. If it doesn't mention a neighborhood, a school, a local business, or a local person — it doesn't meet the standard.

## 3.4 The One-Page Article Formula

**For all publications — web and print:**
- Headline: specific and local, not generic
- Deck: one sentence that tells them exactly what they're getting
- Opening: one paragraph that hooks with a local story or relatable moment
- Body: 4-5 tight paragraphs, each one actionable
- Local source: one quote from a real River Region person
- Close: one sentence with the next action
- Word count: 350-450 words
- With photo: fits perfectly on one print page

## NEWCOMER / FAMILY GUIDE — STRATEGIC DECISIONS (April 30, 2026)

### Brand and naming

- **Master print/digital title:** "The River Region Family Guide"
- **Subtitle on cover:** "Your trusted resource for raising kids in Montgomery, Prattville, Wetumpka, Pike Road, Millbrook, and Eastchase"
- **Positioning line:** "The only guide families turn to — whether you're new to the area or have lived here for years"
- **Web URL:** `/newcomer-guide` (primary, for SEO capture on "moving to" searches)
- **Web URL aliases (redirect to primary):** `/family-guide`, `/new-to-river-region`, `/newcomer`
- **Menu label in main nav:** "Family Guide"
- **Inside framing:** Sections still use newcomer language where appropriate ("Your First 30 Days Here," "If you're new to the River Region…"). Newcomer positioning is preserved as content theme, not as the brand cap.

### Format decisions

- **2026 (June press deadline):** Family Guide stays embedded in June issue of River Region Parents magazine. June 2026 ships as strongest version yet of the embedded guide using Option B (category cleanup + Schools section sponsor + Pediatric Care section sponsor).
- **2026 web launch:** Permanent `/newcomer-guide` page on riverregionparents.com goes live alongside June print. Lives in main navigation. Continuously updated. Articles added rolling through summer/fall.
- **2027 evolution (planning starts Q4 2026):** Standalone 9x6 annual publication. Distributed via partner network — Realtors, hospitals, Chamber of Commerce, school admissions offices, military relocation desks, OBGYN offices. Annual print run, evergreen content, lives on partner desks for 12 months.

### Content philosophy

- **Web is the asset that compounds; print is the curated showcase.** The digital Family Guide is the long-lived publication. Print issues each June pull forward the strongest digital content + the directory + Featured Listings.
- **Advertiser quotes are the editorial engine.** Existing advertisers are interviewed via short question prompts. Their answers become article content and elevate them from "advertiser" to "expert featured by River Region Parents." Continuous content factory at near-zero cost.
- **Categories grow over time.** Web has unlimited capacity. Editorial pieces get added rolling — first batch ships by June press, additions through summer/fall. June 2027 print is built off the strongest content from 12 months of digital guide.

### Audience pattern this enables

- 11 existing guides (Newcomer, Birthday, After School Activities, Special Needs, Fall Event, Holiday Happenings, Education, Healthy Families, Summer Camp, Child Care, Summer Fun) all migrate to the same pattern over time: each becomes a permanent menu item with its own evergreen destination URL, refreshed seasonally for print but lived-in continuously online.

### Active span

- **Family Guide is evergreen — 365 days a year.** Unlike seasonal guides (Summer Fun, Holiday Happenings) that lose meaning when the season passes, the Family Guide is always relevant because families relocate, change schools, have new babies, and start new chapters year-round. This justifies premium 12-month advertiser commitments and higher rates than seasonal guides.
